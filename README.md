# 🎓 Bachelor's Thesis in Computer Science

This thesis presents the development of a **meeting management system** for a recruiting platform, created during an external internship at NESECOM SRLS in Milan from March to June 2024, as part of my Bachelor's degree at the **University of Milano-Bicocca**.

The system is designed to help companies **organize and manage online interviews with candidates** efficiently. It allows companies to schedule meetings, add participants, and handle notifications, ensuring all communications happen directly through the platform in a secure and structured way.
